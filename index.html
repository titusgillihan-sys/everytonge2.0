Every Tongue

Scripture Integrity Song Finder

In tonal languages, the pitch of a word isn't tone of voice — it's part of the word's meaning. When Scripture is set to a melody, the tune can force the voice onto pitches the language doesn't want, quietly distorting the words being sung. That matters because song, not text, is how many oral, low-literacy communities actually learn and remember Scripture. Every Tongue finds the existing, vetted translation and existing melody where the tune fights the language the least — and flags exactly where it doesn't.

Two hard constraints

No generated translation

Every word shown comes from an existing, published, vetted translation — never AI-authored Scripture.

No generated melody

Every tune is an existing, known melody. Any wording swap is a human-reviewed suggestion, never auto-applied.
